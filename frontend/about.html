ABOUT STUDENTSTAY

A safer way to find a room in Greater Noida.

How it works

You see the photos and the sector first. The street address, the map, and the phone numbers stay hidden.

When you are ready, you pay the broker fee. It is half of one month’s rent. You scan one QR code and upload the receipt.

After the admin approves that receipt, you can open the house, call the landlord, and call the admin.

Landlords post their own rooms. The admin is the broker: one QR code, one person to reach.
