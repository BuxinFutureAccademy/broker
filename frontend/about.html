Who we are

AiDoBot Technologies is us. The company site is aidobot.in. We are students at Sharda University, and we started the company to put useful technology in people’s hands.

Our first product is a medical screening device. Ekvora is the next thing we are building: a clear way for international students in Greater Noida to rent a home.

The healthcare robot

AiDoBot is an AI-powered screening robot. It checks heart rate, oxygen, temperature, blood pressure, weight, and height, and it is built to speak the person’s language, including English and Hindi.

The idea is simple. A person walks up, the robot takes the screening, and a doctor can see the result. It is meant for hospitals, clinics, community centres, and homes, so a basic health check does not always mean a long trip to a clinic.

Face analysis, a live doctor call, and medicine delivery are part of the same robot. The full picture is on aidobot.in.

Why we made Ekvora

We are international students in Greater Noida. Finding a trustworthy room was the problem we wanted to solve next.

On this site you see the photos and the sector first: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, the Knowledge Parks. The street address, the map, and the phone numbers stay hidden.

When you are ready, you pay the broker fee. It is half of one month’s rent. You scan one QR code and upload the receipt. The next step is to contact the broker and send that receipt. The street, the map, and the landlord’s number stay private.

Landlords post their own rooms. We are the broker. There is one QR code and one team to reach.

Questions

What do I pay for a house?

The broker fee is half of one month’s rent for that house. You scan the one QR code on the house page and upload a photo of the receipt. After the admin approves it, you contact the broker and send that receipt.

Why is the street still hidden?

Photos and the sector are public. The street, the map, and the landlord’s phone stay private. Paying the broker fee does not open those.

How do I order from a restaurant, a shop, or a service?

Sign in as a student, open the menu, and place one request. The order stays on that menu. The owner accepts it, then types the amount received. You confirm that you received it, that you did not receive it, or you send a note. You can place another request of that same kind only after you answer.

Can I order food and request a service at the same time?

Yes. A meal, a shop purchase, and a service request are separate. You still finish the open one of that kind before you start another of the same kind.

What if someone is dishonest?

Owners and buyers tell the truth about the money and about whether the order arrived. Ekvora can remove a dishonest owner’s restaurant, shop, or service, and can remove a dishonest buyer’s account. The full rule is on the Privacy and policy page.
