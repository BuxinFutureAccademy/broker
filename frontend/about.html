Who we are

AiDoBot Technologies is us. The company site is aidobot.in. We are students at Sharda University, and we started the company to put useful technology in people’s hands.

Our first product is a medical screening device. StudentStay is the next thing we are building: a clear way for international students in Greater Noida to rent a home.

The healthcare robot

AiDoBot is an AI-powered screening robot. It checks heart rate, oxygen, temperature, blood pressure, weight, and height, and it is built to speak the person’s language, including English and Hindi.

The idea is simple. A person walks up, the robot takes the screening, and a doctor can see the result. It is meant for hospitals, clinics, community centres, and homes, so a basic health check does not always mean a long trip to a clinic.

Face analysis, a live doctor call, and medicine delivery are part of the same robot. The full picture is on aidobot.in.

Why we made StudentStay

We are international students in Greater Noida. Finding a trustworthy room was the problem we wanted to solve next.

On this site you see the photos and the sector first: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, the Knowledge Parks. The street address, the map, and the phone numbers stay hidden.

When you are ready, you pay the broker fee. It is half of one month’s rent. You scan one QR code and upload the receipt. The next step is to contact the broker and send that receipt. The street, the map, and the landlord’s number stay private.

Landlords post their own rooms. We are the broker. There is one QR code and one team to reach.
